Yantar Archive · local tool / 0.47

Observation Log

A compact record of facts, reports and hypotheses with filtering and plain-text export.

Purpose

Separate what you witnessed from what someone said. Record source, level and in-game time. The log retains a bounded number of entries to avoid unlimited growth in one browser profile.

Limit

The archive does not read ALife events and is not a live threat map. You are responsible for the accuracy of entries; nothing is published.

Local worksheet

JavaScript is needed for this local tool. Articles remain readable.
