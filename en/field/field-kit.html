Beyond the Perimeter · field memo 14 / 0.47

Field kit: a decision, not a shopping list

Judge equipment by the route you can complete and retrace, not by price or the number of objects in your pack.

Let the task define the load

Choose one objective and one cancellation condition before departure. “Reach the station shelter and return before dark” is measurable; “see what is out there” invites needless weight. Short reconnaissance values mobility; field repairs require tools and time. If you cannot carry everything essential, narrow the objective.

Three separate reserves

Keep immediate-use supplies, return supplies and delay reserves apart in your plan. Medical supplies reserved for the journey home should not be counted as available for an opening firefight. Items hidden in a remote stash are not an emergency kit until you can reach the stash.

Compatibility before rarity

Check ammunition type, weapon condition, recovery options and protection for the specific hazard. Item statistics and availability vary between Anomaly installations. This article does not read your configuration or inventory and therefore invents no numeric equipment ratings.

Decide when to retreat

Write down two withdrawal triggers before leaving, such as using the last medical reserve or becoming too heavily loaded to move freely. They should still apply when rare loot appears ahead. Reassess the route, daylight and supplies after the first major encounter.

At the campfire

Use the Departure Board to review the plan. The record stays in your browser profile; it neither consumes equipment nor creates missions. The guide works without internet access.
