Beyond the Perimeter · field memo 15 / 0.47

Inspect a shelter before the alarm

A roof does not automatically mean protection. A useful refuge needs a known approach, a verified shelter mechanic and a fallback.

A room is not necessarily a safe zone

In Anomaly a sturdy-looking building may not be recognised as emission shelter. Use the signs your actual game installation provides. This website cannot certify any particular room and does not claim to supply guaranteed safe coordinates.

Check the approach

Study the entrance in ordinary conditions. Is there an alternate path? Can you recognise it without a lamp? Could a door, staircase or hostile group block it? In an emergency, a long detour may be worse than retreating to a known location.

Keep an alternative

For every long leg of travel, record at least one place to stop or turn around. Do not tie survival to a single doorway. A fight or migrating mutant group may change access after your last visit.

Night and timing

Consider visibility, exit routes and the remaining reserve as well as shelter. A site that felt manageable by day may not be suitable at night. Date reports from other stalkers; yesterday’s sighting is not a promise about tonight.

Recording evidence

In a route card distinguish “personally checked” from “reported”. Record landmarks and how you verified a shelter rather than writing “100% safe”. Verify uncertain cases in the actual game.
