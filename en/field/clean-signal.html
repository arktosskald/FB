Open Frequency · protocol 09 / 0.47

Clear signal: a four-line radio report

A message helps only when its recipient can tell what was heard, what was verified and what action is being requested.

Line one: location

State the level and a recognisable landmark. Do not invent numeric coordinates that you never checked. When speaking publicly, do not expose someone else’s shelter without permission.

Line two: time

In-game observation time is not the same as a server publication timestamp. “Just now” goes stale faster than “before the emission”. Record how precise the time estimate really is.

Line three: source

“Saw” means you witnessed it; “heard” describes a sound, not its cause; “reported” requires attribution and the source’s level of certainty. A howl does not prove that a particular mutant has appeared.

Line four: purpose

Say whether you want help, verification or only to warn travellers. A sentence on a website cannot create an in-game contract or payment; those belong to the game’s existing contract system.

Example

“Garbage, northern fork. Near dusk. I heard automatic fire beyond the hill, but did not see the shooters. Check the approach before bringing a group.” This is more useful than an unsupported red circle on a map.
