Open Frequency · local tool / 0.47

Rumour Review Card

Break down a report by source, age and independent confirmation. The conclusion is a checking aid, not a game probability.

Principle

This tool does not compute hidden gameplay odds. It only indicates how well the report was documented. Even a carefully documented witness may be wrong.

Revision

Update the card after checking a claim. Several retellings of the same story are not multiple independent confirmations.

Local worksheet

JavaScript is needed for this local tool. Articles remain readable.
