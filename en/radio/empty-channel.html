Dead Channel 17: a transcript

The archive holds static, fragments of two phrases and a dispute between radio operators. It offers no secret quest or guaranteed signal.

00:12 — first recording

The operator wrote: “Rain started… do not enter…”. Crackling swallowed the rest. No bearing was recorded. One radio hand later guessed it referred to a tunnel, another to an old warehouse; neither had heard the decisive words.

00:19 — second recording

The next tape contains a short tone and a click. It could be a nearby transmitter, a fault or another attempt to call. Nobody demonstrated a connection to the first phrase.

Disagreement at the station

A guide wanted the path closed for a day. The duty officer refused to issue an official warning without a location or time. The editor kept both accounts: uncertainty does not eliminate the need for care, but it cannot be converted into a definite map marker.

Archive traces

The paper card says “channel 17” without identifying the equipment. Different radios use different channel plans. Typing the number into the game will not unlock a scene; this is a fictional F&B network document.

What remains useful

Quote recordings faithfully, distinguish theories from facts and seek a second check on important warnings. The danger in this story is the certainty that grows from an incomplete tape.
