Three witnesses to one psi signal

Three people described the same disturbance in different ways. None of their accounts should automatically become the final verdict.

The operator

A station operator logged a short burst of static in his headset and several seconds without contact. He did not determine the source or direction. The record says “unidentified interference”, not the name of a creature.

The guide

A guide returning from a route heard a voice that seemed to call his name. He saw nobody and could not identify the direction with confidence. He was short of sleep, but that neither makes his account false nor proves anything supernatural.

The newcomer

A newcomer heard a cry and assumed a mutant was nearby. Later he retold the assumption as confirmed fact. The editor preserved the original wording: “no animal seen, no independent second observation”.

Summary

Their times roughly coincide but their explanations do not. A common event is plausible; the cause remains unknown. Future investigators need the conditions and time of observation more than a dramatic label on a map.
