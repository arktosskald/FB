Perimeter Market · listing review / 0.47

Lot 041: a battery with three past lives

One casing, three incompatible stories and a practical marketplace rule: verify what you are paying for.

The listing

“Field battery, almost new, taken from a German radio set. Runs for a week. Trade for a rare tool.” The next day its owner called it a spare medical-device part; a day later, a trophy from a northern expedition. No proof of origin accompanied any of the stories.

Warning signs

Conflicting stories do not prove theft, but they reduce confidence in promises. No opportunity to check the charge, an unusually attractive bargain and pressure to decide immediately all make the exchange riskier. Compatibility depends on the exact item and game build.

Before a trade

Identify the item using the game’s actual data. Check whether the current installation provides condition testing, then decide whether to buy. This site cannot inspect the trader’s stock and does not claim that the item is presently for sale.

Why the editor kept it

Market stalls attract both honest sellers with confused stories and deliberate fraudsters. Learning to ask better questions is more useful than treating everyone with a suspicious tale as guilty.

Result

The archived listing is marked unverified. No purchase is possible on this page: money and inventory may change only through trusted in-game UI.
