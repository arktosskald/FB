Perimeter Market · local tool / 0.47

Supply Ledger

Plan a quantity for each supply category and enter what you actually carry. The tool displays the difference.

Purpose

This is a browser-based paper ledger, not an inventory scanner. Categories are general because specific medical items and ammunition vary by Anomaly build.

Reading the result

A shortage is only the difference between two numbers that you entered. Zero shortage is no guarantee of safety and excessive stock can impair mobility. Use the ledger to plan, not to predict survival.

Local worksheet

JavaScript is needed for this local tool. Articles remain readable.
