Three readings do not make a map

A lab technician’s note on the difference between an instrument reading and a confident legend about the “edge of an anomaly”.

Log 18/A

The first operator recorded a signal near a concrete ring. A second reading was taken after the wind changed, with a different device. The third was reconstructed from the memory of a traveller hurrying back to camp. On paper all three looked identical until the conditions were compared.

What the instrument cannot know

A detector reading is an observation under particular conditions, not a guarantee that a passage stays safe. Record the device, moment, direction of travel and method of verification next to any number. Detectors and anomalies behave differently across modded Anomaly builds.

Errors of alignment

If one witness says “by the tree” and another says “behind the wall”, they may have measured different spots. Plotting three unreliable points does not make a reliable boundary. This archive preserves uncertainty instead of filling gaps with a convenient outline.

Repeat-visit protocol

Choose a safe point of departure, record each action and repeat the observation when conditions allow. Flag changes after an emission. Never value a risky test above the ability to return; the log is for later decisions, not proof at any cost.

Editor’s note

This is fictional educational material. Its data are not taken from your save, do not spawn anomalous zones and cannot replace actual observations in the game.
